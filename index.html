About
I am a master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Stanford, where I work on memory and control for vision-language-action models in the Multi-Robot Systems Lab. Before Stanford I studied Mechanical Engineering at UC San Diego, where I worked on nanophotonics and structural color in the Poulikakos Lab.
